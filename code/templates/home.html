{# ───────────── +1 Speed Catch Animals ───────────── #}

Roblox game, in development, built for phones first

+1 Speed Catch Animals

A Roblox game I'm building with a small team. You train your speed, catch speedy animals and race other players.

Plus one, again and again.

Training adds speed a little at a time. You level up, unlock boosts and watch the number climb.

Catch it to move on.

Every stage has a speedy animal. Get fast enough, catch it, and the next stage opens. Stage 1 starts with a chicken at 100 speed.

You become the animal.

Reach an animal's speed and you turn into it. Your character grows as you rank up.

Then race anyone.

Tap to race other players over three laps. Bosses, pets, crates and rebirths keep it going.

Read about the game

How it works: real play-test captures, then 24 frames rendered from the game's 3D model and drawn to a canvas. Drag to spin the cow.

{# ───────────── TopSecret ───────────── #}

Java and SQLite team project

TopSecret

A team Java app for Intro to Problem Solving, with logins, passwords and an audit log. I built the user roles and access levels.

You only see what you're allowed to.

Menu options a user can't access stay hidden.

The first account runs the place.

The first person to register becomes the administrator. Everyone after starts as a standard user.

Admins manage everyone else.

An admin can promote and demote other users.

But never themselves.

An admin can't demote their own account.
